HalloWeekends: Dudes After Dark

October 22, 2026 · Cedar Point

Three Coasters. One Legendary Night.

The Dudes returned to Cedar Point for another HalloWeekends adventure, and this year we went big!

Armed with Fast Lane passes, we tackled Steel Vengeance, Maverick, and Top Thrill 2! For several of the Dudes, this was their first time experiencing Top Thrill 2.

Between the roller coasters came Halloween fog, a couple rounds of adult beverages, questionable conversations, and plenty of laughs.

We wrapped up the evening in the rocking chairs, doing a little people-watching before breaking with tradition and trading our annual corn dogs for Gargoyle Wings!

Three major coasters. A few first-time thrills. Plenty of laughs. Another successful Dudes mission!
