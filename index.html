Built in Driveways. Powered by Friendship.

Dudes in Driveways started with a simple belief: adult friendships matter. Life gets busy — careers, families, responsibilities — and connection can quietly slip to the bottom of the list. This group exists to keep it intentional.

We gather. We explore. We learn. We laugh. It started in a driveway, and it keeps going because it works.

About Us

Dudes in Driveways began as a simple idea: neighbours meeting in their driveways for a casual drink and chat. Over time it has grown into a welcoming community where everyone is invited. We hold regular gatherings throughout the year, fostering connections and making our neighbourhood stronger.

Whether you're new to the area or have lived here your whole life, we hope you'll join us at our next driveway event. Take a look at our upcoming calendar, browse photos from past gatherings and learn how to organise your own event.
