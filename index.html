Built in Driveways. Powered by Friendship.

Dudes in Driveways started with a simple belief: adult friendships matter. Life gets busy — careers, families, responsibilities — and connection can quietly slip to the bottom of the list. This group exists to keep it intentional.

We gather. We explore. We learn. We laugh. It started in a driveway, and it keeps going because it works.

About Us

Dudes in Driveways started in Ohio in 2022 with a simple goal: make it easy for guys to stay connected. Not networking. Not another obligation. Just real friendships, built over shared experiences.

We also like to support local businesses along the way. A lot of our events are built around places in our own backyard — whether it’s a restaurant, a venue, or something we might not have checked out otherwise. It’s a chance to learn something new, start conversations, and support the people building things right here in our community.

Some months it’s a driveway hang. Other times it’s a tour, a show, a game night, a museum, a road trip, or something we’ve never tried before. The point is to show up, do life together, and leave with more than small talk.

New here? Start with the upcoming calendar, check out the Past Events archive, and if you want to launch a chapter in your own town, we’ll help you get it rolling.
