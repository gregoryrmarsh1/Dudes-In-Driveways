Caves, Trains & Twin Lakes

May 17, 2026 · Underground caverns, railroad history, and an improvised stop at Twin Lakes.

Some events go exactly according to plan. This wasn't one of them.

We started underground at Seneca Caverns, spent time exploring the Mad River & NKP Railroad Museum, and when our original dinner stop turned out to be closed, we improvised and headed to Twin Lakes Golf Course instead.

As usual, the destination mattered less than the company.
