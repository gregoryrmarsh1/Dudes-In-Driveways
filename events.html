I’m In Venue Info

Pickleball, conversation, and a little friendly competition — an easy one to jump into after work.

The Plan

We’re heading to The BackYard Pickleball & Social Club for an evening of pickleball, conversation, and a little friendly competition.

It’s a newer spot, and the owner has offered to give us a quick tour and share how the place came together, which should make for a cool behind-the-scenes look before we get rolling.

Whether you’ve played before or not, this one should be easy to jump into.

Schedule

  • Date: Wednesday, April 22, 2026
  • Location: The BackYard Pickleball & Social Club
  • 5:30 – 7:30 PM: Pickleball (rotating in and out, all skill levels)
  • 7:30 – 8:30 PM: Beer wall + hang
  • After: Cleats for dinner for anyone still in

What to Expect

  • Indoor courts and a relaxed setup
  • No experience needed
  • Easy rotation so everyone gets time in
  • A mix of activity and conversation

Come for all of it or just part of it.

Simple. Social. Consistent.
