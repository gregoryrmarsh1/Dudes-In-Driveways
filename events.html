Mission Briefing

Dudes, we've been called into service.

The Huron Salvation Army has entrusted us with a red kettle, a handful of bells, and two hours to make some serious holiday magic happen.

Our mission?

Make December 16 the single biggest Red Kettle donation night of the holiday season in Huron!

And we're not showing up quietly.

Remember those giant speakers you used to haul into garage parties in the '90s? Bring 'em.

That strobe light your wife said you'd never use again? This is its moment.

Got a spotlight powerful enough to summon Batman? Perfect.

Christmas music. Hot chocolate. Camping chairs. Holiday inflatables. Kids. Wives. Parents. Friends. Neighbors.

BRING. IT. ALL.

We're turning the sidewalk outside Cornell's into Dudes in Driveways Holiday Headquarters.

This isn't just another monthly gathering. This is our chance to give back to the community that has given us so much.

The 10-Minute Bell-Ringing Challenge

Every Dude has a mission:

Give us at least 10 minutes of your finest bell-ringing performance!

When the group text goes out, throw your hat in the ring. Let us know you're coming and roughly when you'd like to take the bell.

No spreadsheets. No complicated schedules. Just Dudes stepping up.

During your 10 minutes, rally your friends, family, coworkers, and neighbors to swing by Cornell's.

Have them drive up, drop some Christmas cheddar into the kettle, and head on their way.

Better yet, encourage them to stick around and ring alongside you!

Want to ring for 20 minutes? Great.

Want to hang out for the entire two hours? Even better!

The goal isn't to fill time slots. It's to fill that kettle.

Operation: Completely Unnecessary Christmas Production

Somewhere in your basement is a box of Christmas lights.

Somewhere in your garage is a speaker system that hasn't been used since 1997.

And somewhere in your house is a holiday decoration your wife has specifically asked you not to put in the front yard.

December 16 is your moment.

We're talking Christmas music, inflatable Santas, hot chocolate, camping chairs, ridiculous hats, and enough holiday spirit to make Clark Griswold proud.

Have a strobe light? Bring it.

A spotlight that could summon Batman? Absolutely.

An 18-inch speaker from your garage-party days? It's time for its triumphant return.

We're not promising that any of this is necessary.

We're just saying that if we're going to ring a bell, we're going to ring the heck out of it.

And somewhere in the middle of all that nonsense, we're going to raise some serious money for Huron families who need a hand this Christmas.

Let's keep the festivities welcoming, family-friendly, and safe for shoppers. Decorations, music, and equipment should stay clear of entrances and walkways. We'll coordinate larger setups with the store and Salvation Army.

Why We Ring

Here's the part that really matters.

The money raised by the Huron Salvation Army stays right here in Huron.

It helps local individuals and families who are facing difficult circumstances.

  • 💡 Utilities: Helping families keep the lights and heat on.
  • 🏠 Housing: Helping people keep a roof over their heads.
  • 🎒 School Fees: Helping families cover important school expenses.
  • 🚗 Car Repairs: Helping residents handle essential transportation needs.

According to the Huron Salvation Army, the Red Kettle campaign is their only annual fundraiser, and volunteer bell ringers are their greatest need.

Think about that.

We've spent the year enjoying everything our community has to offer.

We've explored local businesses, visited interesting places, shared meals, made memories, and strengthened friendships.

Now it's our turn to give something back.

Our goal is ambitious: make this the biggest Red Kettle donation night of the holiday season in Huron.

We may not know the final total when the evening ends, but we can make sure we give this mission everything we've got.

Call in the Reinforcements

This isn't just for the Dudes.

We want the whole community to know what's happening.

Tell your parents. Bring your wife and kids. Invite your coworkers. Call your neighbors.

Have your friends stop by during your 10-minute bell-ringing window.

They don't even need to get out of their cars if someone can safely meet them with the kettle in an appropriate area.

They can make a donation, say hello, and be on their way.

Or they can stick around, grab a bell, and join the fun!

Every visitor is another chance to make a difference.
