July 16–19, 2026 · Sandusky, Ohio

Mission Details Ironman 70.3 Ohio

Music. Volunteering. Racing. Cheering. Swimming. Food. Friends. And hopefully a finish line.

Mission Briefing

For reasons still under investigation, the City of Sandusky appears to have organized an entire weekend of activities around the Dudes in Driveways Ironman 70.3 Relay Team.

City officials claim the timing is purely coincidental. We remain unconvinced.

Join Greg, Jonathan, and Jim as Team Dudes in Driveways takes on the 2026 Ironman 70.3 Ohio Relay. Whether you join us for one event or the entire weekend, this is shaping up to be one of the biggest Dudes gatherings of the year.

Thursday, July 16

  • Event: Mallrats Concert
  • Time: 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM
  • Location: Jackson Street Pier

We’ll kick off the weekend with live music downtown at Jackson Street Pier. The band is Mallrats, so expect a fun throwback summer-night vibe.

Mallrats Band Website

Saturday, July 18

  • Event: Volunteer Opportunities
  • Status: Optional

Anyone interested can help support the race weekend by volunteering. Details will be shared once the best options are locked in.

Sunday, July 19

  • Event: Ironman 70.3 Ohio Relay
  • Team: Dudes in Driveways
  • Swim: Greg
  • Bike: Jonathan
  • Run: Jim

The main event. Come cheer, heckle responsibly, and witness three middle-aged Dudes attempt something that seemed like a better idea when we signed up.

Sunday Recovery

  • Event: Recovery Pool Party
  • When: After the race

After the finish line, we’ll shift into recovery mode with a pool party, food, drinks, and a full review of questionable athletic choices.

Join Us

Come for the concert, volunteer, cheer on race day, join the recovery party, or jump into the whole weekend.
