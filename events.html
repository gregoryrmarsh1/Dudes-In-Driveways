Upcoming Event

Odin’s Airsoft & Shamrock

Sunday, March 15, 2026 · Norwalk, Ohio

I’m In Venue Info

Indoor airsoft. Team strategy. Controlled chaos. Show up ready to move and leave with stories.

The Plan

March Dudes Day is going active. We’re heading to Odin’s Paradise for an all-day indoor airsoft run. Huge field. Big weekend turnout. Fast-paced games.

Plan is to arrive around 9:00 AM to handle waivers and rentals. First games usually kick off around 10:00 AM. Come for the whole day or jump in for part of it.

Quick Details

  • Date: Sunday, March 15, 2026
  • Where: Odin’s Paradise (Norwalk, OH)
  • Arrive: 9:00 AM (waivers + rentals)
  • Games: ~10:00 AM start

Cost & Gear

  • Around $25 to play
  • Rentals available if you don’t have gear
  • If you do have your own setup — bring it
  • Wear comfortable clothes + closed-toe shoes

Safety briefing provided on-site.

Dudes + Kids

Kids are welcome. Minors need a signed waiver (ages 10–12 require a parent present). This one can be multi-generational if you want it to be.

Lunch & Aftermath

Bring a cooler or lunch for the day. Late afternoon, we’ll roll over to Red Shamrock in Norwalk for dinner and the debrief — because the stories are usually better than the scores.
