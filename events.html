Behind the Scenes:
Huron City Hall

Tuesday, November 17, 2026 · 6:00 PM

Mission Details City of Huron

City Hall. Behind closed doors.
A look at what keeps Huron running.

Mission Briefing

This November, the Dudes are heading somewhere many of us have driven past hundreds of times, but may have never really explored.

Huron City Hall.

We live here. We work here. We raise our families here. We drive on the roads, use the parks, pay the water bills, and occasionally wonder who makes all the decisions around town.

Well, this month, we're going to find out!

Join us for a behind-the-scenes evening with Huron City Manager Stu Hamilton, who has agreed to welcome the Dudes to City Hall for a conversation about how our community operates.

We'll learn a little about what a city manager actually does, how City Council and city staff work together, and what it takes to keep Huron moving forward.

And because this is Dudes in Driveways, we're not interested in just sitting through another meeting.

We want to see some stuff!

The goal is simple: meet the people, explore the places, ask the questions, and walk away knowing a little more about the community we call home.

Mission Details

  • 📅 Date: Tuesday, November 17, 2026
  • 🕕 Time: 6:00 PM
  • 🏛️ Location: Huron City Hall
  • 📍 Address: 417 Main Street, Huron, Ohio
  • 👤 Host: City Manager Stu Hamilton
  • 💵 Cost: Free
  • 👟 Dress Code: Casual. Comfortable shoes recommended.

Why City Hall?

Think about it.

How many times have you driven past City Hall without ever stepping inside?

Have you ever attended a City Council meeting? Met the city manager? Seen where city decisions are discussed or where residents go to take care of city business?

For many of us, the answer might be no.

That's what makes this a perfect Dudes in Driveways adventure.

Sometimes the most interesting places to explore are right in our own backyard.

The Game Plan

We're keeping this one informal, interactive, and flexible.

Stu has agreed to host us at City Hall, and we're hoping to get a closer look at some of the people and places that keep Huron running.

  • 🏛️ Meet the City Manager: Hear from Stu about his role, working with City Council, and managing city operations.
  • 🗣️ Ask the Questions: Learn how decisions are made, how residents can get involved, and how city departments work together.
  • 🚶 Explore City Hall: Take a look around the building and see where city business happens.
  • ⚖️ City Council Chambers: Get a closer look at the place where council meetings and public discussions take place.
  • 🚓 Police Department: If arrangements allow, perhaps we'll get a behind-the-scenes peek at the adjoining police facilities.
  • 🚒 Fire Department: If available, we may have an opportunity to see some of the equipment and facilities used by Huron's firefighters.

The exact tour stops will depend on city operations, staffing, and availability. Police and fire department visits are possibilities, not confirmed stops.

Three Things You Never Knew About Huron

Here's a challenge we've given Stu:

If you could show residents three things about how Huron operates that they might never otherwise get to see, what would they be?

Maybe it's something inside City Hall. Maybe it's an interesting piece of equipment. Maybe it's a story about how a city service works behind the scenes.

Whatever he chooses, we're looking forward to learning something new.

No elaborate presentation required. Just a good conversation, a little exploring, and the opportunity to ask questions.

Getting to Know Our Community

One of the things Dudes in Driveways does best is bring people together through shared experiences.

This month, we're taking that idea one step further.

We want to make local government a little more approachable.

The people working at City Hall, the police department, and the fire department aren't strangers in some faraway government office.

They're part of our community.

And maybe after this evening, we'll all feel a little more comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, and getting involved.

Another Door Opens

During our conversations with Stu, another interesting possibility came up.

The Huron Water Treatment Plant.

Ever wonder where our drinking water comes from, how it's treated, or what happens behind the scenes before it reaches our homes?

Stu suggested that the Water Treatment Plant might make a great separate Dudes in Driveways visit.

So we're saving that idea for another adventure.

One city. Plenty of places to explore.

Bring Your Curiosity

You don't need to know anything about city government to join us.

In fact, that's kind of the point.

Have a question about city services? Curious how City Council works? Wonder what the city manager actually does all day?

This is a great opportunity to ask those questions directly.

We're not heading to City Hall to debate politics or solve every problem in Huron.

We're going to learn, explore, and hopefully leave with a better understanding of our hometown.
