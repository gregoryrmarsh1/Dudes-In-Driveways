Odin’s Paradise – Tactical Airsoft Night

March 15, 2026 · 6:30 PM · Odin’s Paradise Indoor Airsoft Arena

Indoor airsoft. Team strategy. Controlled chaos. Show up ready to move and leave with stories.

What It Is

This month we’re trading the driveway for controlled chaos.

Odin’s Paradise gives us a chance to compete, move, strategize, and test our coordination in a fast-paced indoor airsoft arena. It’s less about “winning” and more about stepping outside our comfort zone together.

Expect teamwork. Expect laughs. Expect to learn who takes this way too seriously.

Why This One Matters

Every season looks different. Some months are reflective. Some months are relaxed. This one is active.

Part of building camaraderie is doing something that pushes you just enough to feel it the next day.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothes you can move in
  • Closed-toe shoes
  • Competitive spirit (optional but encouraged)
  • A willingness to try something different

Equipment and safety briefing provided on-site.

Afterward

We’ll grab food nearby and debrief — because half the stories come after the event.
