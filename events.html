Mission Details Coastal Swing

Indoor golf. Cold drinks. Hot pizza. Questionable swings.

Mission Briefing

September takes the Dudes indoors for a night of golf, drinks, pizza, and athletic confidence that may or may not survive first contact with the ball.

We have reserved time at Coastal Swing in Sandusky from 6:00–8:00 PM for two hours of indoor golf simulator action.

We will rotate through the simulators, take some swings, talk entirely too much about technique, and order Chet & Matt's Pizza from next door.

Golfers can show off. Non-golfers can give it a shot. Everyone else can grab a slice and provide completely unsolicited swing advice.

What Is Fairways & Pizza Pies?

Think less "serious golf outing" and more "a bunch of Dudes standing around a simulator convinced the next swing is going to be the good one."

Coastal Swing gives us a chance to play golf indoors, try different courses, compete a little, and hang out without worrying about weather, daylight, or finding twelve consecutive good golf shots.

No golf experience is required.

Seriously.

How the Night Works

We have the simulators reserved for two hours, so the plan is simple: show up, grab a drink, pick up a club, and rotate through as the evening goes.

Depending on how many Dudes join us, we may play a few holes, hit the driving range, run some closest-to-the-pin challenges, or simply see who can produce the most questionable shot trajectory of the evening.

  • 🏌️ Take some swings
  • ⛳ Try the golf simulators
  • 🎯 Challenge another Dude
  • 🍕 Grab some pizza
  • 🍺 Enjoy a drink
  • 😂 Offer swing advice nobody requested

Scorekeeping is optional. Bragging rights are inevitable.

The Pizza Plan

One of the best parts of this month's mission is that Chet & Matt's Pizza is right next door.

We will order pizza for the group during the evening and bring it over to Coastal Swing so nobody has to choose between taking another swing and eating another slice.

Hot pizza. Indoor golf. Cold drinks.

We may have accidentally created a pretty good system.

Golf Simulator Notes

  • No golf experience is necessary.
  • Come ready to rotate through the simulators with the group.
  • Golfers are welcome to bring their own clubs if they would like.
  • Non-golfers are encouraged to take a few swings anyway.
  • Competitive behavior should remain mostly unnecessary.
  • Trash talk should remain completely unnecessary but is expected.

Chance someone suddenly believes he has fixed his golf swing: Extremely High

Chance the simulator immediately proves otherwise: Also Extremely High

No Golf Skills Required

This is not a tournament and nobody needs to know the difference between a birdie, bogey, fade, draw, or whatever excuse golfers use after hitting the ball sideways.

If you golf, great.

If you have never golfed, even better.

Take a few swings, eat some pizza, grab a drink, or simply hang out and watch the rest of us discover how accurately a simulator can measure disappointment.
